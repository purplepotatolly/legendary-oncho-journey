Pre-boosting score — boost only what earns it

A creative-quality metric applied to TikTok and Instagram posts before boost budget is committed. Version 2 integrates Google's ABCD creative principles — attention, branding, connection, direction — as the creative-quality layer, on top of the trend-context layer from the content trend framework. The premise is unchanged: strong creative riding a rising, unowned trend multiplies boost spend; strong creative pushing a crowded trend buys reach the brand was getting anyway. Sample window: January – March 2026.
Scores, ABCD sub-scores and before/after deltas: modeled illustrative data — refresh from live Meltwater creative review before client use. Post URLs are the real samples supplied by P&G.

The pre-boost score v2 — creative quality × trend context, 0–100

Creative quality — ABCD
40% weight · 4 × 10 pts
The post is scored on the four ABCD principles below, adapted from paid video ads to organic creator content. Creative execution is the single biggest driver of campaign effectiveness — score it before spending on it.
Trend position
30% weight
Which trend does the creative ride, and where does that trend sit in the content trend framework? Content opportunity earns full points; crowded space earns few — do not boost into saturation.
Engagement quality & velocity
15% weight
Likes, shares, saves and comments per view — saves and shares weighted above likes as stronger commerce-intent signals — plus first-48-hour growth against the creator's baseline.
Comment relevance
15% weight
Percentage of comments relevant to the brand or category (e.g. Pantene or haircare). High views with off-topic comments signal entertainment value, not commerce value.

The ABCD layer — what each principle asks of the creative

Each principle scores 0–10. Guidance below is tuned to the Pantene Japan brief; the same rubric applies to Downy Philippines with its own distinctive assets.
A
Attention — hook the viewer
0–10 pts
Earn engagement from the first seconds: open mid-action or on a close-up (damaged hair, the treatment moment), bright strong visuals, and sound-on audio — music, voice-over, texture sounds. No slow build-ups.
B
Branding — early, often, richly
0–10 pts
Work Pantene into the story early and repeatedly — product and pack shots, the gold capsule as the distinctive visual asset, spoken brand mentions. Organic feeds are sound-on: audio branding counts.
C
Connection — think or feel something
0–10 pts
Educate, inspire or entertain — humanised, focused storytelling with emotional levers like surprise. Pantene JP's proven arc is exactly this: "昔のイメージ" skepticism turning to surprise. One message, not many.
D
Direction — ask for action
0–10 pts
One clear, simple next step: try the new treatment, tap the basket, save the routine. Text overlay, voice-over or an end-card scene. Weak D is the most common — and most fixable — failure in organic creator posts.
Creative principles adapted from Google's ABCD framework for effective video creative (Think with Google), developed with Ipsos and independently validated by Nielsen and Kantar. On average the ABCDs deliver a 30% lift in short-term sales likelihood and a 17% lift in long-term brand contribution (Google/Kantar Link AI, global, 2021). Adaptation to organic creator content, weighting and trend-context layer: Meltwater.
The boosting rule v2: score ≥70 with the trend in content opportunity → boost. Score 50–69 → hold; if the gap is a weak D, fix it — add a CTA overlay or end-card — and re-score at 72 hours, because direction is repairable before spend. Score <50, or any post riding a crowded-space trend → do not boost; no ABCD score can rescue a saturated trend, the budget moves to a higher-scoring asset. Track every metric before and after boost per post — the deltas train the next round's threshold.
