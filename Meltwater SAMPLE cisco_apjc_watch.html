  • Evidence source: Meltwater Internal news retrieval, 16–23 September 2026. Individual record links are retained where the retrieved source supplied a usable public URL.

  • Classification: “Named competitor / incumbent” is used only where the record explicitly names Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, HPE Juniper or Arista. Other rows are opportunity triggers and are labelled accordingly.

  • Watch taxonomy: Networking: Cisco, HPE/Juniper, Arista; AI networking, data centre, campus, Wi-Fi, SD-WAN, Ethernet AI fabric, AIOps, automation, cloud region and GPU expansion. Security: Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet; SASE/SSE, AI security, SOC/XDR/SIEM, Zero Trust, identity, DLP, ransomware, sovereignty, GenAI governance and consolidation.
